# ST7-odd — the five 2.4.10 level-3 oddities (2026-10-13)

**Branch:** `pilot/ST7-odd-2411` (from `origin/main` 19dbbbcc, release 2.4.10). **Source:** `docs/release/2.4.10-INTEGRATION-NOTES.md`,
"Studio 6 before / after", the oddities the integrator saw in the merged round-3 images. Each was reproduced with the opt-in
look-dev harness (`frontend/tests/lookdev-studio6.spec.ts`, `SW_LOOKDEV=1`, the house of `tests/lookdev/house.ts`) and the real
specs, traced to a cause, fixed there, and given a regression test. Before = the committed round-3 "after" images
(`docs/design/studio6/round3/after/`, the 2.4.10 code); after = `docs/design/studio6/odd-2411/after/` (this branch, same frames).

## 1. Root causes and fixes

| # | Oddity (as seen) | Root cause | Fix | Regression test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Translucent blue "ghost" volumes spilling outside the outer walls in the iso and top views | Not the upper storey and not the presence plates: the **camera cones**. `scene-builder.anchors` draws a cone as a prism from the floor to the camera's mount height (2.4-2.5 m in the house) and `scene-three.single` gave it the shared level-2 accent material, which the section cut of level 3 never clips (`RealMaterials.setClip` reaches the realistic materials only). The walls are cut at 0.6 x the ceiling (1.68 m), the prism kept rising to 2.4 m: the iso projects that extra 0.7 m of translucent volume over and past the cut walls, and the top preset is a **perspective** camera (`setPreset('top')` uses `this.persp`), so a tall prism near the plan's edge projects outward past the walls | Each cone prism owns its material (`userData.cone`, like the tints' `ownMaterial`) and `applyCut` gives it the section plane with the walls, lifting it with the dial, in a camera preset and in the walk exactly as the walls. Levels 1-2 are untouched (`localClippingEnabled` is false there) | `unit-plan-3d-odd.spec.ts` "1+2": both house cones carry the plane at 1.68 m, two distinct materials; dial off / camera preset -> none; top preset -> back; level 2 -> none |
| 2 | White wisps above the wall tops in the replay day image (near the camera icon and the right-hand corner) | Two causes. (a) The same cone prism: by the camera body a translucent column rose from the cut to the mount. (b) The faint grey diamonds over the far wall corners, and a smear above the camera, were the **GTAO pass**: `GTAOPass` renders its own normal / depth with a `MeshNormalMaterial` that carried no clipping planes, so the occlusion of the **full-height** walls (strongest at their top corners and round the camera body) was composited over the sky above the cut walls - the realistic rung only (the lite rung has no GTAO), hence "wisps" in the realistic day image and none in the lite ones. The same pass drew the faint pale haze hugging the hall's outer walls in the realistic iso | (a) the cone cut above; (b) `PostChain.setClip` hands the section plane to the GTAO normal material; `applyCut` calls it with the walls and a chain that arrives after the cut gets it on creation (`SceneView.cutPlanes`). Cutting that pass uncovered a second GTAO fault: the ground seen just over a cut wall's top lies metres behind it and the pass darkened it against the wall top as a grey band along every far wall (SwiftShader and the workstation GPU alike), so the backdrop - sky dome, horizon band, ground plate, contact shade - is hidden for that pass only (`SkipAoPass` pair around `GTAOPass`, `userData.skipAO`): as background (depth 1) the AO shader leaves it alone, and the building's contact with the ground stays the radial shade it always was | `unit-plan-3d-odd` "1+2" on the realistic rung: `view.postClip` is `[1.68]` with the cut, null with the dial off, back with it on; the backdrop meshes carry `skipAO` and are visible after a drawn frame; the regenerated `history-l3-realistic-day-*` baselines (section 3) |
| 3 | The kitchen door pill clipped under the terrace shutter pill at 390 px | `sw-plan-3d.layoutOverlays` placed every pill on its projected point with no collision handling; the two anchors (the sliding door at the living / kitchen wall, the terrace shutter on the east wall) project 9 px apart in y in the phone iso view | `pill-layout.ts`: a per-frame screen-space pass over the shown pills (at most the cap: 60 / 8) - from the lowest on the screen up (the nearer one in an overhead view keeps its place), a pill that would cover an already placed pill is raised to sit 2 px above it. Pill sizes are read once per text (no layout read per frame). Chips (room temperatures) are not moved | `unit-pill-layout.spec.ts` (node, 5 tests: apart, the door / shutter pair, a stack with a deterministic order, 60 random pills never overlap, unknown sizes); `unit-plan-3d-odd` "3" on the phone (the four alerting pills of the cap, the kitchen door and the shutter among them, no pair overlapping) and on a desk (ten pills). Against the 2.4.10 build the phone test fails with exactly `binary_sensor.kitchen_door / cover.living_terrace` |
| 4 | A wall screen covering the window in the office walk | The look-dev house hung the office whiteboard (`board`, 1.8 m) on the south wall over `win-off-s`; the same fixture defect twice more: the living-room screen (`tv`) over `win-liv-n1` / `win-liv-n2` and the meeting-room board (`mboard`) over `uw-n1`. A fixture fault, not a product one (the product draws what the plan says) | `house.ts`: the office board moved to the kitchen wall (x = 7.5, 1.5 m), the meeting board to the east wall, the living-room screen wall to the west wall between the two doors (sofa, table, rug and lamp turned to face it) | `unit-lookdev-house.spec.ts` (node): every wall-mounted item (hung >= 0.5 m, <= 0.15 m thin) stays clear of every opening on its level - across the wall within 0.35 m and along it past 5 cm from a jamb while their heights overlap; on the 2.4.10 fixture it names the four offenders |
| 5 | The "ייצוא glTF" link half under the "סיור" control | Measured at 1440 / 1080 / 1024 px on the dist preview with the walk chip present: the bar's children never overlap (glTF 554-640, walk 646-691 at 1440). What the integrator saw is the **ghost** `sw-button` (transparent surface, `--sw-text-2` text) drawn over the rendered picture: in the realistic iso frame the ground plate's shadow edge runs under it, so half the text sat on grey and half on dark - it read as cut off | The export control is a surfaced `sw-button` like the camera select and the chips beside it (`variant="ghost"` dropped; `--sw-surface` / `--sw-border-strong` from the component, no new rule, no new token) | `unit-plan-3d-odd` "5" on desktop / tablet / mobile: no two bar controls overlap, the control is not `ghost`, its computed background and border are opaque |

Budgets: no new chunk import (`unit-three-chunk`), no new skin rule (the change is inside `sw-plan-3d`'s own styles; the five skins' rule files are untouched), only `--sw-*` tokens (`unit-token-lint`), the quality ladder / `dpr_cap` / the R178 perf table untouched. The pill pass costs O(n^2) on at most the cap per drawn frame (3 600 rectangle checks on a 60-pill desk).

## 3. Linux pixel baselines

Regenerated on the Linux test machine (`~/pixodd.sh`, own worktree `~/work/pix-st7-odd`, the tests lock shared, `npx playwright test
tests/evidence-replay-3d.spec.ts --update-snapshots=all`, desktop / tablet / mobile, 27 passed) at `2ba14947` and looked at as old | new
pairs, every one of the 12 `evidence-replay-3d` baselines (the only pixel spec that draws a level-3 orbit view; the gate's
`pixel-stable` set - 188 passed, `pixel-walk-touch` included - did not change):

| Baseline | What changed (old -> new) |
|---|---|
| `history-l3-lite-day-{desktop,tablet,mobile}` | the translucent column of the cone above the corner camera (2.4 m against the 1.8 m cut) is gone; nothing else moved (diff box 60 x 62 px at the camera) |
| `history-l3-lite-night-{desktop,tablet,mobile}` | the same column, fainter at night, gone |
| `event-l3-lite-{desktop,tablet,mobile}` | the event card's small picture: the same column by the camera, gone (diff box 25 x 27 px) |
| `history-l3-realistic-day-{desktop,tablet,mobile}` | the column, the grey diamonds over the far corners and the smear above the camera are gone (cone cut + GTAO cut); the ground plate no longer carries screen-space occlusion (it was a soft dark zone round the building, now the contact shade alone) - the wider diff; walls, floors, furniture, caps and pills are the same |

The first rewrite (cone cut alone) left the realistic baselines within the comparison tolerance (`maxDiffPixelRatio`), which is
why the wisps had never failed the pixel stage; `--update-snapshots=all` rewrote them so the committed pictures are the real ones.
