## Known items for the gate / owner

  scene probe) at 200 KB; the post-processing chain is its own lazy chunk (50,187 B). Together, when the realistic rung loads
  both, they are 207,750 B gzip - above 200 KB. The spec measures them separately (as the branch designed); whether the 200 KB
  budget means the `three` chunk alone or the realistic load total is an owner / spec question.

  is NOT_RUN.

  S0's default) - the still when the floor has one, otherwise the last frame - and wakes on a touch or a device change; the
  CHANGELOG's "nothing changes by itself" paragraph says so.
