**Request (owner, 2026-09-22/23):** load a plan, calibrate it once, draw or automatically detect walls, doors and windows, place objects from a searchable, extensible library (sports, hospital, safety, lighting and more), set ceiling heights and two levels on one floor (a sports hall whose tribune goes down to a lower level), group several lamps on one switch, and see all of it in 3D with the cameras and Home Assistant entities — embedded in the product's own map, not a separate tool — then export it. DWG and BIM (IFC) as later phases.
**Decision:** a versioned geometry layer (canonical document v2: walls, openings, objects, circuits, levels, connectors) on the existing plan versions, rendered by the existing map canvas on every map surface (live floor map, editor, history map at time t, event page, the Lovelace card that embeds the floor screen); live things stay map anchors and library objects are their physical bodies; local detection first, AI plan analysis stays behind T060/T061 consent and budget; three.js as an on-demand chunk inside the map screen; DWG through an isolated converter process and IFC through a dependency-free writer (import via IfcOpenShell after an availability check) in phases 7–8. Design: `docs/architecture/PLAN_STUDIO_DESIGN_HE.md`.
**Scope impact:** requirements R167–R180 and tasks T084–T090 (V2; T084–T087 P1, T088–T090 P2 — owner decision 2026-09-25: the necessary core is phases 1–4, phase 6 comes before phase 5, and phases 6, 5, 7, 8 in that order stay future development proposals, so T088 (phase 5) moves from P1 to P2 alongside T089 (phase 6) and T090 (phase 8); see `docs/architecture/PLAN_STUDIO_DESIGN_HE.md` section 14); T065 becomes phase 7 (DWG) and now depends on T086; T084 implements T059's acceptance (R117/R118).
**Editor tool added (T087 follow-up, 2026-09-29, branch `pilot/T087-door-tool`):** the structure tool's **"סמן דלת"** mode (key D) - recommendation 1 of `docs/evidence/T087/DOOR_MODEL_STUDY_2026-09-29.md`, since automatic door detection fails on the owner's scans. One click on a door symbol (or on the wall where a door is) asks `POST /plan-versions/{id}/door-proposal` (map.edit; `{x, y}` in 0..1, optional `wall_id`, `level_id`), which reads a small crop of the version picture at its upload resolution in the detection worker pool (5 s guard, 504 `door_proposal_timeout`; nothing stored, nothing audited, no picture leaves the server) and proposes the wall, position, width (a 90 degree leaf's length, a slanted leaf's or double door's jamb-to-jamb gap, a bare gap on a drawn wall, else the default 0.9 m), hinge side and swing from the symbol - arc, chord ("triangle"), 30-60 degree leaf, arc or "V" double door. The editor shows it as a ghost with its note ("נמצאה קשת" / "נמצא פער" / "ברירת מחדל - בדוק" ...) and three handles (width drag, hinge flip, swing flip); Enter or a click on it accepts, a click on the next symbol accepts it and proposes the next, Esc cancels. The result is an ordinary opening (one undo step). **Decision (click off every wall):** a symbol found with no drawn wall along it is proposed with a short wall piece of its own (door width + 0.12 m each side, the wall's measured thickness) and a warning; a click with neither a symbol nor a drawn wall near it is refused with a message (422 `no_wall`) - no door without a wall. On the owner's three scans, 10 of the 24 sampled doors come out right without adjustment (floor 0: 2/8, floor -1: 2/8, floor -2: 6/8; with or without calibration). Service `smplwise/services/plan_door_tool.py`, editor `frontend/src/map/door-tool.ts`.
**Unchanged:** the normalized 0–1 geometry contract, metres only after calibration (owner decision 2026-09-23: before calibration the editor shows estimates marked "≈" by default; the setting `plan.estimates` = false hides metres until calibration), nothing automatic published without a person, map-first UI, VMS authorization (map.read / map.edit / map.publish / map.import, plus a new catalog.manage), backups and audit for every change, no production writes to devices.
