# LOGOKIT-295 - the approved SmplWise logo kit, everywhere

Owner decision 2026-10-24 (developer kit `smplwise-developer-kit-v1`, the approved 02B-2 design with the rounded w and the thin SW symbol).

## What changed
- `frontend/src/design/sw-logo-art.ts` is GENERATED by `scripts/brand/gen_sw_logo_art.py` from `docs/brand/logo-kit/svg` (the kit's brand / white / navy / black SVGs): the two outlines (SW symbol, smplwise wordmark), the viewBox and transform per layout, the fill per tone. One function, `swLogoSvg(layout, tone, alt)`, returns inline SVG; `<sw-brand kind="product">` and the notice-board TV bundle both use it.
- Dark surfaces use the white tone and carry no plate or box: the logo is transparent and sits on the screen's own background.
- Placements: top bar light = horizontal/brand, dark = horizontal/white; rail and phone mark = symbol; sign-in page = stacked (`stacked` property); notice-board TV day = horizontal/brand, night and take-over = horizontal/white; kiosk wall header = horizontal/white; PDF headers = the kit's `logo-horizontal-1024.png` (brand tone, transparent) as `assets/bill/brand/smplwise-word.png`.
- The browser tab icon stays the Arx icon (`icons/arx.svg`, `icons/arx-192.png`); the PWA manifest and the mobile launcher icons are untouched. `public/brand/smplwise-mark.*` and `scripts/brand/render_smplwise_logo.py` are removed; the mint `smplwise-logo.png` was already gone.
- TV: the logo box is 30 px high (26 in portrait), the width follows the kit's ratio.
