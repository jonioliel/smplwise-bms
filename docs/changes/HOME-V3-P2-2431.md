## What changed

Every capability is hidden when its module is switched off, when the person lacks its permission, or when the administrator turned the
person's home category off (new categories: `presence`, `calendar`, `notices`, `tickets`, `schedules`; admin page labels added). The
home interface never grants anything: each route checks the module's own permission.

### Owner decisions implemented

* **Who is home:** the household sees each other (anyone holding `presence.report` - whoever takes part with a phone). The placement is
  THE rule of "who is in the building" (`presence_people.place_device`). A person who hides themself is never listed to anyone else, not
  even counted; they see themself as "מוסתר מהאחרים". Hiding needs no permission (it only restricts); showing again needs
  `presence.report`. Nobody can set it for another person (same opt-out list as the full interface; audited `presence.people.opt_out`
  with `via: home`). No floor, area or device leaves the route.
* **Tickets:** a resident sees only the tickets they OPENED (`creator` in the visibility reasons; a member's department tickets stay in
  the full interface). Create = the tickets module's own `create_ticket` (idempotent request id, its audit and notifications); note =
  its own `add_note`.
* **Long notice:** the notice body scrolls only inside its sheet (`.n-body`); every list pages.
* **Schedules:** only schedules whose every action is `scene.turn_on` of one scene are listed or touched; creation builds a scene-only
  draft on the server (`home_family.scene_draft`) and calls the schedules module's own `ops.create` (permission, local-identity rules,
  bridge write, audit). A person without `schedule.manage` sees the list read-only.
* **Wall panel locked to a room:** the room screen adds "לוח הודעות" and "יומן" categories; both open read-only (no new event, no
  booking tab, no new ticket on a locked panel).

### Server

* New `services/home_family.py` + `routers/home_family.py` (all under `/home/`, the home-only prefix). `GET /home/family` = the feature map.
* Home-only allowlist (`services/home_interface.py`): phase 1's GET-only list now also holds `/notices`, `/notices/module`,
  `/calendar/calendars`, `/calendar/occurrences`, `/calendar/rooms/day`, `/calendar/module`; exact writes `/calendar/events`,
  `/calendar/availability`, `/automations/scene`; patterns `^/calendar/events/<id>/(cancel|withdraw)$` and `^/notices/<id>/read$`.
  Calendar administration (maintenance, connections, feeds, guests), notice administration (boards, publish, reads), `/tickets*` and
  `/schedules*` stay refused for a home-only person (tested).
* "Do not disturb": table `home_notify_quiet` (migration 0214); the phone push channel (`mobile_push.AppPushChannel`) skips a person's
  push with reason `dnd` inside their window, never for critical severity, an escalation, `safety`, `security` (alarm) or `doors`.
  The inbox, e-mail and other people are unaffected. Muting a kind writes the existing per-phone `push_muted_json` on ALL the caller's
  own phones (a critical kind is never muted).
* Weather: the TV feed's block plus feels-like, wind, rain chance and the raw condition per hour / day (rain is no longer drawn as a cloud).
