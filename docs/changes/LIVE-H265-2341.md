# LIVE-H265-2341 - live video of H.265 cameras in browsers that cannot decode H.265

Status: implemented on `fix/LIVE-H265-2341` (from `origin/release/2340-train`); not merged, no version bump. Code and
tests only: no device was contacted (no NVR, no go2rtc, no Home Assistant).

## Why

The azia lab, 2026-11-01 (read-only measurement, `private/planning/AZIA_LIVE_DIAG_2026-11-01_HE.md` section 7): all 32
`smplwise_nvr-1_chN_{main,sub}` streams in go2rtc returned a frame, yet in the browser some cameras played and some did not.
The recorder's API reports H.265 on channels 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11 and 15. Chrome and Edge do not decode H.265 over
WebRTC (Safari can on some versions); over MSE they decode it only with hardware HEVC support. Those tiles stayed black.

## What changed

1. **Codec per stream.** The camera registry (`capabilities_json.encoding`, filled by the discovery; for Provision from the
   cached GetStreamCaps / stream encodings of `fix/AZIA-LIVE-2331`) gives each profile's codec; `services/live_codec.py`
   folds the vendor spellings (H.265+, SmartH265, HEVC) into `h264 | h265 | mjpeg | other`. When the registry does not know
   a profile, the codec go2rtc's producer reported after a first connect is used (`go2rtc.producer_video_codec`: the
   `receivers[].codec.codec_name` of go2rtc 1.9, else the `medias` strings). `GET /media/live/{id}` reports `codecs` and the
   policy; the camera admin table (הגדרות ‹ אבטחה ‹ מצלמות) shows an H.265 stream as a small badge with a Hebrew hint.
2. **Browser capability.** `frontend/src/api/h265-support.ts`: `RTCRtpReceiver.getCapabilities('video')` (WebRTC) and
   `MediaSource.isTypeSupported('video/mp4; codecs="hvc1..."' / "hev1...")` (MSE). Each live socket carries `h265=1|0` for the
   transport of that attempt. An older client sends nothing and gets today's behaviour.
3. **Fallback order** (server side, `live_codec.choose`, per attempt) when the requested stream is H.265 and the attempt's
   transport cannot decode it:
   - (a) the channel's other profile when it is H.264 (usually the sub stream);
   - (b) else an H.264 variant transcoded by go2rtc: stream `smplwise_<recorder>_ch<n>_<profile>_h264`, source
     `ffmpeg:smplwise_<recorder>_ch<n>_<profile>#video=h264` - one of our streams, named after the camera's own stream, its
     source naming only our own stream (no address, no credential). Created through the same `Go2rtc.ensure_stream` as every
     stream, only when a viewer needs it;
   - (c) else the socket is refused (`h265_unsupported`, close 4415) and the player moves to the next step of its chain - MSE
     when the transport choice allows it and the browser decodes H.265 there (an `auto` chain whose browser decodes H.265 over
     MSE only starts on MSE: that is "plays as is", no transcoding). An explicit "WebRTC only" choice still never plays MSE
     (owner rule 2026-10-01). There is no HLS path in Arx today; none was added;
   - (d) else the tile shows "הדפדפן לא תומך בקידוד H.265 של המצלמה; אין זרם חלופי" over the snapshot instead of a black
     picture (no retry). A fallback that plays shows a small tag ("זרם משני" / "H.264 · תרגום") whose tooltip is
     "הדפדפן לא תומך בקידוד H.265 של המצלמה; מוצג זרם חלופי"; the full sentence also shows when "הודעות על אופן ההזרמה" is on.
   The server announces its choice in an `arx/stream` text frame before go2rtc's signalling (a player that does not know it
   ignores it).
4. **Load budget.** Every fallback is a normal live session (counted by `media.max_live_sessions`, the wall's budget and the
   remote cap as before). Transcoding is capped by `media.h265_max_transcodes` (default 2, 0 = never): distinct transcoded
   streams at once; more viewers of one transcoded stream share go2rtc's single ffmpeg. Beyond the cap the socket is refused
   (`transcode_cap`, close 4430) and the tile keeps a snapshot refreshed every 10 s with the notice "...ומכסת התרגומים במקביל
   (N) מלאה; מוצגת תמונה מתעדכנת", retrying the live socket with the player's usual back-off (3 s .. 30 s).
5. **Setting** הגדרות ‹ וידאו ומדיה ‹ "קידוד H.265 בדפדפן" (`media.h265_policy`): אוטומטי (default, the order above) / תמיד
   זרם משני (only (a)) / תמיד תרגום (only (b)) / כבוי (the stream as it is). Plus "תרגומים במקביל" (`media.h265_max_transcodes`,
   0..16). Both apply only to a stream the browser cannot decode; validated by `SettingsPatch` (422 otherwise).
6. A removed or disabled recorder's `_h264` streams are deleted with its other streams (exact names, our namespace).

The recorder's encoding is never changed. Nothing outside the `smplwise_` namespace is created, changed or deleted.
