# NOSCROLL-1366-2437: thirteen control screens fit 1366x768 and 1440x900

Branch `pilot/NOSCROLL-1366-2437`, cut from `origin/main` (v2.43.1, `ab7894837`). Owner rule 2026-10-24: a control screen never
scrolls. Input: the design audit of round 30 (2026-11-12, `private/design-system-2026-10-27/round30/system-audit/`, finding #5,
FIXES.md items G1, E6, I5, D4, H6, C9; owner question 7 answered "fix all: only an inner panel scrolls"). The audit measured at
1366x768 seven screens whose page (the shell's `<main>`) scrolled, two multimedia screens whose own element scrolled, and four
screens whose whole content scrolled inside one page-sized panel.

Layout only: no colours, contrast, type, terms, tokens or shared components were changed (those belong to
`pilot/SYS-AUDIT-QUICKFIX-2437` and to the shared-component branch). No shell file, no `styles/devices-themes.ts`, no badge tokens.

## 1. Method

The approach of NOSCROLL-297 / OVERVIEWFIT-297 / WARMUP-POLISH-2421, applied per screen: from the desktop edge the screen takes the
height the shell gives it (`block-size: 100%`, rows `minmax(0, 1fr)`); then density, a secondary panel beside or collapsed instead of
below, and **pages instead of scrolling** for card grids that hold more than fits (`sw-pager`, rows per page measured after layout
with a ResizeObserver; a page always holds whole rows). Below the desktop edge every new wrapper is `display: contents` or the rule
does not apply: tablet and phone keep their layouts.

Desktop edge: `(min-width: 1024px)`; generator and temperature `1101px` (at 1024x768 paging left two cards a page and the counters
wrapped, so the tablet keeps its layout); WisKey stations `1100px` (the screen's existing desktop edge); multimedia also
`(min-height: 560px)`.

## 5. Known limits

- The board at 1366x768 shows 6 pages for the 12 mock requests (cards are 200-260 px tall; the today row and the banner take ~140 px).
  Smaller cards are a card-design decision (tickets branch C).
- Camera health keeps the recorder card as a compact row (FIXES G1 suggested a tab or drawer); some empty room is left above it.
- Multimedia screens pages 3 cards a page at 1366x768; fitting two floors needs smaller cards (`components/media-*`, branch D).
- Generator charts view was not measured (the guard covers the dials view).
- Tablet 1024x768: generator and temperature keep the scrolling tablet layout (edge 1101 px).
- Pixel baselines of the visual matrix (`devices-home`, `live-wall`, `security-alarm`, `multimedia-screens`) and the WisKey stations
  pixel specs change and are re-taken on the runner in this branch.
