## Endpoint

`GET /api/v1/cameras/{camera_id}/recordings/days?month=YYYY-MM`

- Permission: `video.playback` on the camera (the same check as the day search: 403 before 404).
- Month: a local month of the installation's IANA zone (`time.zone`); `month` must match `^\d{4}-(0[1-9]|1[0-2])$` and
  lie in 2000-2100 (422 otherwise).
- Answer:
  `{camera_id, month, timezone, days: ["YYYY-MM-DD", ...], source, coverage, through, searched_at, note}`
  - `source`: `daily_distribution` (Hikvision), `segment_search` (Hikvision fallback), `record_dates` (Provision-ISR),
    `future` (a month after today: the device is not asked).
  - `coverage`: `complete` | `partial` (partial = a fallback search window hit the page cap: days may be missing; never
    reported as "no recordings").
  - `through`: the last local date that was asked (today in the current month; `null` for a future month).
- Errors: 409 `no_track` (camera without a recording track), the recorder's own 503 / 409 errors as for the day search.

Device reads (all read-only queries; nothing is written to any recorder):

1. **Provision-ISR**: `SearchRecordDate` through `vendor_io.record_days` (existing `ProvisionPlayback.record_days`),
   filtered to the month and to days up to today.
2. **Hikvision**: `POST /ISAPI/ContentMgmt/record/tracks/<main_track>/dailyDistribution` with the legacy body
   (`<trackDailyParam><year>…</year><monthOfYear>…</monthOfYear></trackDailyParam>`); the device answers per day of
   its wall-clock month, which is the installation's local month (the same NVR-clock assumption every Hikvision search
   here makes, KNOWN_QUIRKS T2).
3. **Hikvision fallback** when dailyDistribution is not offered (non-200 other than 401/403, a `ResponseStatus`,
   unparsable XML, or a day list without days): derived from the existing recording search
   (`recordings.search_segments`) in at most five windows of up to seven local days, contiguous in wall clock (DST-safe,
   by the zone rules), the window holding today clipped to now; every segment marks each local date it touches.
   Each window is also cached by the day-search cache.

Multi-NVR: every read goes to the camera's own recorder (`recorder_scope.camera_settings`). Cache: per (camera, month,
zone) - 60 s for the month that holds today, 1 h for past months; 256 entries, oldest quarter dropped; concurrent
identical requests share one device read. `recorded_days.invalidate(camera_id)` clears it.

Files: `smplwise_vms/backend/smplwise/services/recorded_days.py` (new), `services/nvr.py` (`daily_distribution`,
`parse_daily_distribution`, `DailyDistributionUnsupported`), `services/recorders/vendor_io.py` (`record_days`),
`routers/recordings.py` (the route). `contracts/API_INVENTORY.md` is regenerated by the release train's version commit
(`scripts/api_inventory.py`), not here.

## UI

`frontend/src/components/sw-recorded-month.ts` (new) + `recorded-month-logic.ts` (pure logic), wired into the playback
screen (`screens/investigate-playback.ts`, SC12) beside the date field, in the existing toolbar style (the 38 px
field-height button; 44 px on touch / narrow layouts):

- A calendar button opens a month card: month title with previous / next month (next is disabled at the current month),
  Sunday-first week in RTL, days with recordings in the recorded accent with a dot under the number (shape as well as
  colour), the selected day filled, today ringed, future days disabled, past days "checking" (a soft pulse; none with
  reduced motion) until the answer arrives - never shown as empty before that.
- Tapping a day opens that day (`setDate`, the same path as the date field) and closes the card; Escape / an outside tap
  closes it and returns focus to the button.
- A summary line: "N ימים עם הקלטות", "אין ימים עם הקלטות בחודש זה", or the partial warning; an error line with "נסה שוב".
- Keyboard: arrows walk days (direction follows RTL), PageUp / PageDown months; one roving tab stop; each day has a Hebrew
  accessible name (weekday, date, recorded / not, today).
- No scrolling inside the card (owner rule): the grid always reserves six weeks, so the card never changes height.
  Desktop: a top-layer popover under the button, its inline-end edge on the button's, kept inside the viewport.
  Phone (<= 600 px): a bottom sheet with 44 px day targets.
- One loader per camera: switching the camera clears the card's month cache. The demo skeleton (no backend) shows a
  fixed September 2026 so the screen can be reviewed.

Design pass: the installed `impeccable` skill (context + craft floor; its detector on the new component: no findings);
tokens only (`--sw-menu-surface`, `--sw-accent-soft`, `--sw-recorded-text`, `--sw-shadow-3`, ...), the menu surface and
entrance of `sw-dropdown`.

Workstation look (Vite dev server, demo mode, Chromium in the Browser pane): desktop 967 px card anchored under the
button, no horizontal overflow; 375 x 812 phone bottom sheet (0 to 375 px, bottom 812), card scrollHeight = clientHeight
(408 = 408), page scrollWidth 375. One defect found and fixed during the look: an earlier desktop anchor stayed on the
card when the same element later opened as a phone sheet (the inline left / top are now cleared for the sheet). A second
fix: a tap on the button while the card is open closed it and opened it again (light dismiss on pointerdown + click);
the button now remembers that the card was open at the press.

## Known limits

- The Hikvision month is the NVR's wall-clock month; a recorder whose clock is not kept in the installation zone shifts
  days at the edges (the same limit as the day search).
- Fallback months with very dense recordings can hit the search page cap (25 x 40 matches per window): the answer is then
  `partial`, said in the card.
- The card asks only for the camera chosen in the screen; the synchronized group view has no month card.
