## Acceptance
- **R-T120-1 / AT-T120-1:** A per-person interface policy (default home|full, may switch, home only, wall panel locked to a room with an admin exit code, visible rooms and categories) set by an administrator (rbac.assign) in user management; new regular users open the home interface, administrators the full one; existing users keep the full interface after the upgrade.
- **R-T120-2 / AT-T120-2:** A home-only person is refused (403 home_interface_only, audited) on every API area outside the home interface, on the server; the switch to the full interface is refused when the policy locks it.
- **R-T120-3 / AT-T120-3:** The home interface (room-centred, Hebrew RTL, no Home Assistant branding or internal terms) shows real floors/rooms, lights (dimmers) and on/off switches, climate, shutters, doors and the intercom call, cameras, electricity, alerts and scenes through the existing control paths; room / house off reaches lights and light-typed switches only.
- **R-T120-4 / AT-T120-4:** Favourites per person (an administrator may seed a starting set), favourite rooms, hidden rooms, what opens first, theme; scenes edited by administrators only.
- **R-T120-5 / AT-T120-5:** No scrolling on the control screens at 1280x800, 390x844 and 1440x900; touch targets of at least 56 px on the tablet.
